Acme is a software company founded in 2018.

The company has three main products: Atlas, Nova, and Pulse.

Atlas is a project management platform designed for small teams.

Nova is an analytics platform that helps companies understand
their customer behavior.

Pulse is a customer support platform that provides ticketing
and live chat features.

The engineering team is based in Bangalore and works primarily
with Python and TypeScript.

Acme uses PostgreSQL as its primary database and Redis for
caching.
